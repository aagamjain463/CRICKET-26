Purpose: compare this project against *observable* behaviour of Cricket 26 (Big Ant Studios) so gaps are tracked honestly.

**Rule for this document:** a Cricket 26 entry is only filled in when it has been observed first-hand (own gameplay capture or a cited public video with timestamp). Nothing here was captured yet, so every Cricket 26 cell reads **UNVERIFIED**. Do not replace it with memory or assumption. No proprietary code, assets, UI art, audio or data from Cricket 26 are used in this project.

| Batting (human) | UNVERIFIED | Direction from held keys, intent J/K/L (ground/loft/defend), press timing decides contact (stamped at the frame-interval midpoint — unbiased at 30/60/120 fps, error within half a frame; the delivery itself is resolved analytically at 240 Hz, independent of render rate); resolved through bat geometry — edges, toe, splice, inner/outer half come from where the ball meets the blade. | Fielding | UNVERIFIED | Intercept solver over the ball path per fielder (reaction, acceleration, dive reach), keeper standing up/back, catches, throws, run-outs. T20 presets: pace death (deep point, short third man) / spin defensive, max 5 outside the circle. Fielders take 0.5 s to judge a ball in the air (0.25 s along the ground); catches taken on the run are harder. Coordinator: the primary is the first fielder who can physically reach the ball's path (not the nearest); the next quickest goes to where the ball would be a second later as backup; a boundary rider chases a ball nobody can stop; the keeper comes up to the striker's stumps (3.1 s from standing back at pace, already there standing up) and the bowler goes to the non-striker's. One job per fielder, so no two converge. The throw goes to the end where the stumps can be broken soonest (throw flight, direct-hit chance, and whether anyone is there to take it); throwing back across the line they ran costs up to 0.3 s to turn. Fielders move with the solver's own reaction, 6 m/s² acceleration and top speed — the presentation cannot arrive sooner than the solver says. Actions: a fielder who could only reach the ball at full stretch runs on and takes it on their feet when that is quicker than diving and getting up (0.5 s), or while a catch is still in the air — without this, 82% of ground takes were dives; in the current sweep 5 of 844 ground takes are diving stops. Ground takes: attacking pickup on the run (ring, slow ball), clean pickup, long barrier (deep, hard-hit ball, almost never fumbled), sliding stop (chasing toward the rope), diving stop; misfields (36 of 844 ground takes in the sweep, 4%) depend on ball speed, the take and Catching. Each take sets how soon the throw goes (pickup on the run 0.36 s … slide 0.88 s, misfield 1.23 s). Catches: flat, skier, low, diving, keeper, on the rope; a catcher running back whose stopping distance (v²/2a) is longer than the gap to the rope is carried over — they flick it to a team-mate within reach (relay catch) or back to themselves, and a failed flick is six. Throws: overarm; underarm flicked out of a pickup on the run inside about 8 m (no set, more accurate close in); long throws lose pace past 35 m, so from about 57 m a free fielder runs to the line and relays it (in place before the ball reaches them). The HUD summary names the take and throw; dives and slides are shown by the fielder going to ground.  Keeper: every take has a difficulty — standing up, little time to see the ball after it pitched or came off the bat, down the leg side (unsighted behind the batter), at the ankles or above the shoulders, at full stretch — and fumbles cost 0.65 s, which can give byes. ## How to fill a Cricket 26 cell
1. Record gameplay from a licensed copy (or cite a public video with URL + timestamp).
2. Describe only what is visible or measurable (e.g. "ball count resets after a wide: yes/no", "free hit shown on HUD: yes/no").
3. Put the source in the cell. Keep it factual; do not copy UI art or text.

## Benchmark pass (2026-09-23, build `e13ec83`)

Every row was re-checked against the output of this build. The checks were: the headless test run (50 of 50
passing), a 150-delivery in-game soak and the profile runs.

fielding coordinator, the fielding actions and the lofted-drive floor changed what happens after contact, and
they are replaced with this build's measurements.

figures could not be traced to a denominator.
